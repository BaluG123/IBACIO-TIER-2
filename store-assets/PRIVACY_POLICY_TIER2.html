This Privacy Policy describes how the IB ACIO Tier-2 mobile application (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is an independent educational study tool for aspirants preparing for the Intelligence Bureau ACIO Grade-II/Executive Tier-2 (descriptive) examination.

Disclaimer: This App is not affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or connected to the Intelligence Bureau (IB), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India, or any official exam body. All materials are for educational and self-study purposes only.

1. Who we are

IB ACIO Tier-2 is published as an independent exam-preparation app (companion to the Tier-1 app “IB ACIO Exam Preparation”). For support, contact us using the details in the Google Play listing or via in-app WhatsApp support.

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account. We do not sell personal data.

Depending on how you use the App, the following may apply:

  • Data stored only on your device: language preference, writing drafts, practice progress, mock self-scores, and cached daily writing editions. This data stays on your phone unless you clear app storage or uninstall the App.
  • Internet access: used to optionally fetch daily writing prompts / content updates from public GitHub raw URLs, open previous-year paper links (e.g. Google Drive preview), open the Play Store, or open WhatsApp / web WhatsApp when you tap support.
  • No advertising ID collection for ads: the App does not currently show third-party ads or analytics SDKs for tracking users across apps.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, payment card details, or precise location for this App.

3. How we use information

  • To provide exam-prep features (practice, timers, drafts, mocks, daily desk).
  • To remember your language and local progress on-device.
  • To load optional online content you choose to refresh.
  • To help you contact support when you tap WhatsApp / email / Play Store links.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

When you choose to open external services (Google Play, WhatsApp, Google Drive / WebView, GitHub raw content), those services process the request under their own privacy policies. We do not control those third parties.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for exam aspirants and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

6. Data security

On-device storage is protected by your device’s normal security. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Please keep your device updated and avoid sharing screenshots of personal drafts if you consider them private.

7. Your choices

  • You can clear App data from Android system settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time.
  • You can refuse optional network refresh and continue using bundled offline content.
  • You control whether to open WhatsApp, Play Store, or external paper links.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
